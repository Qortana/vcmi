About Us

Transforming Lives Through Christ

Victory Centre Ministries International is a Christ-centered church committed to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ and transforming lives through faith, love, and the power of God's Word.

Our mission is to proclaim salvation through Jesus Christ, build strong believers rooted in faith, and impact our community with God's love. We are dedicated to teaching the Word of God, nurturing spiritual growth, and equipping believers to live victorious lives in Christ.

We envision a church where lives are transformed, families are strengthened, and communities are changed through the power of the Holy Spirit. At Victory Centre Ministries International, everyone is welcome to belong, grow, and serve.
